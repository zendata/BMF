Natal chart

iAngels

by Jikara and Graham

Enter a name, a birth date, a birth time, and a birthplace. iAngels asks for the natal chart and draws the wheel.

Birth date

Use the time people would have read on a clock at the birthplace. If the time is missing, iAngels casts the chart for noon and marks the Ascendant and houses as uncertain.

    Choose a place from the list so the chart has coordinates.

    Planetary positions are calculated by FreeAstroAPI. iAngels draws the wheel and does not recompute the sky. Place search uses Open-Meteo.

    The name stays in this browser. The birth date, time, and coordinates are sent to FreeAstroAPI to cast the chart, and the place name is sent to Open-Meteo. iAngels does not keep a copy.

    Astrology is a symbolic tradition. A chart is not a prediction, and it is not medical, legal, or financial advice.
